# Electricity and Magnetism Labs

This repository contains a collection of laboratory exercises and their analyses pertaining to the field of Electricity and Magnetism. Each subdirectory represents a distinct lab, complete with data, analysis, and reports.

## Directory Structure

Each lab directory typically includes:
- `analysis.ipynb`: Jupyter notebooks containing the detailed analysis of experimental data.
- `README.md` / `readme.md`: Lab specific documentation, procedures, and sometimes results summaries.
- `data/`: Raw experimental data files.
- `images/`: Figures, plots, and other visual aids generated during the analysis or used in reports.

## Labs Included:

- `Building_an_AM_Radio`: Exploration of basic radio principles and circuit building.
- `Charge_to_Mass_Ratio_of_Electron`: Determination of the fundamental physical constant, e/m.
- `Dielectric_Constant_of_Paper`: Investigation into the dielectric properties of materials.
- `Distance_Dependance_of_Radiation`: Study of how radiation intensity varies with distance.
- `Making_a_Transformer`: Principles and construction of transformers.
- `Ohmic_Materials`: Examination of Ohm's Law and material resistivity.
- `Path_Resistance`: Analysis of electrical resistance in various configurations.
- `RC_Circuits`: Characterization of Resistor-Capacitor circuits.

## How to Explore

Visitors are encouraged to:
1.  Navigate into individual lab directories.
2.  Review the `README.md` or `readme.md` files for an overview of each lab.
3.  Examine the `analysis.ipynb` notebooks to understand the methodology and results.
4.  Inspect the `data/` directory to see the raw data collected.
